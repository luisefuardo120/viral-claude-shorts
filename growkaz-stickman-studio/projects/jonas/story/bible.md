# Jonás y el gran pez — story bible

**Logline:** a man runs from God's call, is swallowed by a great fish, prays in the dark and is brought back; you can run from God, but God never runs from you.
**Tone:** drama → wonder → warm triumph. Channel "Cerca de ti" (Christian shorts), Spanish narration, 9:16.

## Locations
- **coast** (golden): low sun, sea band, sand, palms, Nineveh's walls + ziggurat on the horizon. Key: the sun.
- **sea / storm** (dusk → storm): horizon, layered moving waves, the boat with a striped sail; storm adds dark clouds, rain, lightning.
- **under** (deep blue): god rays from the surface, bubbles, kelp silhouettes, dark sea floor.
- **belly** (dark red): ivory ribs, glossy liquid floor, faint red glow; a golden beam when God listens.

## Motifs
Golden light = God (beam, rays, glow on Nineveh). Red arrow = running away. Counter "3 DÍAS" / "DÍA 3" bookends the time in the fish.

## Music
mystery (hook) → hopeful (the call) → tension (escape) → action (storm) → epic (swallowed) → sad (dark) → wonder (heard, third day) → triumph (lesson, end).
