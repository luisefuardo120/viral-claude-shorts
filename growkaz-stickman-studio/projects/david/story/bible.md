# David y Goliat — story bible
**Logline:** a young shepherd with five stones and faith in God brings down a giant who terrified an army; your giant may be enormous, but your God is bigger. **Tone:** epic → tension → light comedy (the armour) → epic faith → triumph. Style: ember (same as Jonás).

## Locations
- **valley** (golden): Israel's hill with tents on the left, the Philistines' hill on the right, the valley floor, a stream for the stones.
- **tent** (interiorWarm): fabric walls, a hanging lamp, spears.
- **void**: the lesson (you vs. a giant shadow, then golden light).
